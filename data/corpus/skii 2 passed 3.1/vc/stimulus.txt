This mobility system can thoughtfully integrate foot pedaling and rubber treads to create intuitive drive sprockets that effectively move seated riders without compromising the rider uprightness. Designed to handle continuous loose particulates, these chassis components seamlessly blend pivoting runners and friction pads to optimize uphill track climbing and ensure rapid safe stopping. To ensure practical manufacturing, hardware components like aluminum tubes, steel chains, and flat baseboards can be configured to guarantee constant track tension and remarkable weather resistance. A highly adaptable steering handlebar allows users to translate arm shifting into fully controlled pathway turning, actively maximizing quick learning while minimizing rider tipping. Ultimately, by exploring distinct pedal layouts and accessible weatherproof plastics, the design delivers a reliable, inventive, and truly affordable tracked cycle.
