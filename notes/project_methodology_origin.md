The thesis methodology was developed in dialogue with **Mengru Wang** (the user the app was built for). The chat record (Apr 1–4, 2026) shows the methodology origin explicitly. Key positions:

**Mengru's framing of the research goal (Apr 1, paraphrased):**
- *"the aim is not to generate something similar to the human one"*
- *"the point of having the system is to have suitable rules of generation, not letting AI guess or mimic structure"*
- *"even if we can have an ideal MC, if it is not well defined structure, then they are generated from the black-box which means random. they can be good but can't be controlled"*
- *"We already have good stimuli. The aim is to find a way we could give rules so that the AI could give stable output … we want a clear route of how AI thinks and generates output from the input."*

This is the thesis's actual research framing: **controllability and reproducibility of AI-generated stimuli**, not output quality per se.

**The 5-sentence locked structure was proposed by Mengru on Apr 2, 1:37 PM,** with theoretical grounding she also provided:

| Sentence | Aspect | Theoretical basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary Function & Core Constraints | Pahl & Beitz (2007) Systematic Engineering Design — task clarification, FBS Function |
| 2 | Operating Flow | FBS Behavior + material/energy flow |
| 3 | Component Architecture | FBS Structure / embodiment design |
| 4 | Ergonomics | Norman (2013) Human-Centered Design |
| 5 | Value Proposition | Ulrich & Eppinger (2015) Product Design Specification |

**The locked-structure principle (Mengru, Apr 2 2:05 PM)** — this is the core methodological constraint of the entire system:
> *"keep same sentence structure, meaning, words among 3 conditions, only the abstraction level of the nouns are different"*

**Why:** This is what makes NC/MC/VC a *controlled* manipulation rather than three separately-generated outputs. Without this constraint, comparison across conditions is confounded by sentence-structure variation, not just abstraction-level variation. The thesis must articulate this principle clearly.

**How to apply:** When writing the methodology section, attribute the 5-sentence structure and the noun-only-abstraction-shift principle to Mengru as research collaborator. Cite Pahl & Beitz, Norman, Ulrich & Eppinger as the underlying framework. The user's contribution is the *implementation* that makes this methodology operational and reproducible.
